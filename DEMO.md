# NeonGate demo note
- **Set up:** plug in a MIDI keyboard and open https://neongate.vercel.app in Chrome or Edge. Tap to start audio and pick your keyboard under MIDI in. Use BPM 100, key A minor, chords `Am F C G`.
- **Play:** press ▶ Play. Hold Am (A C E) with your right hand for a bar, then F, C, G. The Trance 16ths gate chops each chord into 16ths and the pump ducks it on every kick. Ride the **mod wheel** (gate depth) from 0 up to full to bring the chop in. Sweep **CC74** (cutoff) up on the last bar into the loop. Hold **sustain** to let the chords bleed through Halftime Swell.
- **Suggested patch: "Night City":**
  - Voice Neon Saw, cutoff ~1.1 kHz, resonance 6, attack 10 ms, release 350 ms
  - Gate Offbeat Neon, length 80%, depth 95%, pump 50%
  - Drums Four on the floor, scale lock on
  - Switch to Glitch Stutter for the second 4 bars.
- **Record:** press ● Rec, play 2 passes, press Rec again to keep it, then Export WAV + MIDI and drop both on bar 1.
