NEONGATE

A playable gate synth for your MIDI keyboard. Hold a chord and the trance gate and sidechain pump chop it on the grid of your BPM and key.

Tap to start audio.

1 · Your loop

Your BPM, key and chords are saved in this browser and used for playback and export. Changing the key transposes the chords and your take.

2 · Play

bar – · step –

Your MIDI keyboard plays the synth right away and never starts the transport. When the transport is stopped, the gate stays open so you can hear the raw voice. Fallback keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; (Z / X = octave).

4 · Voice

5 · MIDI + audio

Controls: notes + velocity · CC64 sustain · CC1 mod wheel = gate depth · CC74 = cutoff · pitch bend ±2 · CC123 all notes off. MIDI out mirrors what you play (ch 1), the bass (ch 2) and drums (ch 10). No audio input is used.

6 · Export (drops on bar 1 of your DAW)

48 kHz / 24-bit stereo, exactly your bars at your BPM, downbeat at sample 0, ring-out folded into the start so it loops, peak ≤ −1 dBFS. The WAV plays your take (or your chords held one per bar if you haven't recorded one) through the gate. MIDI: tempo, 4/4, key, one track per chair (Gate Synth held notes, Gate Synth gated notes, Sub Bass, Drums).

Every sound in NeonGate is synthesized in your browser and named as a synth. No samples, no accounts, no tracking. © 2026 WorkinWithAI.
